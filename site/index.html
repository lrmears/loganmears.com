01 · About

Defender, puzzle nerd.

I'm a platform security engineer at Gallup. I work with cloud, network and system architects to set security baselines, chase down risk, and keep a close eye on cloud applications, databases and networks through SIEM tooling.

I got here by an unusual route: a music degree first, then cybersecurity. I started hands-on at Blue Cross and Blue Shield of Nebraska, tuning detections in Microsoft Sentinel. I like pattern-finding, which is probably why I'm also hooked on Wordle, linguistics and anything with a cipher in it.
